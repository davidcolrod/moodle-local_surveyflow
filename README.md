# Survey Flow for Moodle

**Survey Flow** (`local_surveyflow`) is a powerful, dynamic, and flexible survey and institutional feedback management plugin designed for Moodle. It enables administrators to build custom survey templates, configure qualitative or quantitative questions (Likert scale), and assign them hierarchically across specific courses or entire course categories.

## Key Features

* **Dynamic Question Builder:** Create and order questions supporting Likert scale measurements (Quantitative) and free-text fields (Qualitative).
* **Hierarchical Publication:** Assign surveys transversally to entire course categories or target specific individual courses.
* **Mandatory Interceptor:** Force mandatory survey participation upon student entry into assigned courses, ensuring high feedback completion rates.
* **Advanced Analytics & KPIs:** Track student reach, participation rates, and view detailed response breakdowns with visual distribution graphs.
* **CSV Reporting:** Export detailed analytical reports directly to Excel (CSV) with full UTF-8 support.
* **International Ready:** Built with a native English codebase and multi-language support (English and Spanish packs included).

## Requirements

* Moodle 4.0 or higher (compatible with modern Moodle releases).
* PHP 7.4 or higher.

## Installation

1. Unzip the `surveyflow` folder into your Moodle root under `/local/surveyflow`.
2. Log in to your Moodle site as an administrator and navigate to **Site administration > Notifications** to trigger the plugin installation.
3. Access the plugin via **Site administration > Survey Flow > Manage Surveys**.
